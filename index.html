Welcome to Hadassah

A school community where learners can grow and thrive.

Hadassah Christian High School welcomes learners seeking a strong foundation for their next stage of education. Based in Opuyo, Soroti East Division, the school follows Uganda’s National Curriculum and offers O’ and A’ Level Arts and Science subjects, with French.

Our aim is to support academic development, practical learning and positive character in a school environment where students can discover their strengths and prepare for the future.
