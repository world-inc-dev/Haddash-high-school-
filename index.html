Welcome to our school

A school community where learners can grow and thrive.

Haddassah Christian High School Uganda welcomes learners seeking a strong foundation for their next stage of education. Based in Opuyo, Soroti East Division, the school offers both the Cambridge Curriculum and Uganda National Curriculum pathways, with O’ and A’ Level Arts and Science subjects and French.

Our aim is to support academic development, practical learning and positive character in a school environment where students can discover their strengths and prepare for the future.
